# Ocarina — Study Context Module

## PURPOSE

This module defines the structure, metrics, and interpretation logic for:

- Concept Test
- Product Test
- InnoTest
- InnoCPT

This serves as the **reference knowledge layer** for all analysis.

---

# 1. STUDY TYPES OVERVIEW

Ocarina recognizes four study types:

### 1. Adhoc Concept Test
Custom concept testing without proprietary modelling.

### 2. Adhoc Product Test
Custom product testing focused on sensory performance.

### 3. InnoTest
Concept testing using Ipsos proprietary RED modelling → Trial Index.

### 4. InnoCPT
Full mix testing (Concept + Product) using:
- Trial Index (Trial)
- Repeat Index (Repeat)

---

# 2. ADHOC CONCEPT TEST

## OBJECTIVE
Evaluate concept appeal and purchase potential.

---

## CORE METRICS

- Overall Liking
- Purchase Intention
- Priced Purchase Intention (optional)

---

## DIAGNOSTICS

Key dimensions:

- Relevance
- Differentiation
- Expensiveness (if priced)
- Believability
- Understandability
- Brand Fit
- Concept Imagery

---

## CONCEPT EVALUATOR (KEY DIAGNOSTIC TOOL)

Respondents identify elements from concept:

### Questions:

1. Most Relevant  
→ What drives resonance

2. Least Relevant  
→ What creates friction

3. Difficult to Believe  
→ What creates credibility issues

4. Unique and Different  
→ What drives differentiation

---

## ELEMENT BREAKDOWN

Each element should be mapped:

- Headline / Tagline
- Insight
- Reason to Believe (RTB)
- Benefit

---

## INTERPRETATION LOGIC

Ocarina should identify:

### Drivers
- Elements appearing in “Most Relevant” and “Unique”

### Barriers
- Elements appearing in:
  - Least Relevant
  - Difficult to Believe

---

## THRESHOLD LOGIC

Element performance is classified as:

- Low
- Moderate
- High

Thresholds are:
- Relative per study
- Not fixed benchmarks

---

## KEY DIAGNOSIS QUESTIONS

- Is the concept relevant enough?
- Is it differentiated enough?
- Is it believable?
- Is price hurting purchase?

---

## WAY FORWARD LOGIC

- Low relevance → strengthen consumer need fit
- Low differentiation → sharpen uniqueness
- Low believability → fix RTB / claims
- Price sensitivity → revisit value vs cost framing

---

# 3. ADHOC PRODUCT TEST

## OBJECTIVE
Evaluate product experience and identify improvement areas.

---

## CORE METRICS

- Overall Liking
- Purchase Intention

---

## DIAGNOSTICS

### 1. Sensory Attributes
- Attribute liking scores

---

### 2. RWA (Relative Weight Analysis)

Purpose:
- Identify importance of each attribute

Logic:
- Higher importance = stronger driver of liking

Dependent variable:
- Usually Overall Liking
- Sometimes Purchase Intention

---

### 3. JAR / Penalty Analysis

Purpose:
- Identify immediate improvement areas

Logic:
- Attributes with high penalty = urgent fixes

---

### 4. Product Image (Optional)

---

## INTERPRETATION LOGIC

Ocarina should identify:

### Drivers
- High importance + high performance

### Critical Weakness
- High importance + low performance

### Low Priority
- Low importance attributes

---

## KEY DIAGNOSIS QUESTIONS

- What drives liking?
- What breaks the experience?
- What needs immediate fixing?

---

## WAY FORWARD LOGIC

- Fix high-penalty attributes first
- Improve high-importance weak areas
- Maintain strong drivers

---

# 4. INNOTEST

## OBJECTIVE
Evaluate concept competitiveness vs MOPP.

---

## CORE METRIC

- Trial Index

---

## DERIVED FROM (RED PREFERENCE)

- Relevance Preference
- Expensiveness (value vs price)
- Differentiation

---

## COMPETITIVE FRAME

- Compared vs MOPP (Most Often Purchased Product)
- Self-defined by respondents

---

## TRAFFIC LIGHT SYSTEM

Each dimension + Trial Index is classified as:

- Solid Green → Excellent
- Hollow Green → Good
- Amber → Moderate
- Hollow Red → Weak
- Solid Red → Very Weak

---

## ACTION STANDARD

- Trial Index ≥ 100 → acceptable (Amber or above)
- Trial Index < 100 → at risk

---

## INTERPRETATION LOGIC

Ocarina should identify:

- Which dimension is limiting Trial
  → Relevance vs Differentiation vs Price

---

## WAY FORWARD LOGIC

- Low relevance → strengthen need fit
- Low differentiation → sharpen uniqueness
- High expensiveness → improve value perception

---

# 5. INNOCPT (FULL MIX)

## OBJECTIVE

Evaluate:
- Trial (Concept strength)
- Repeat (Product strength)

---

## CORE METRICS

- Trial Index
- Repeat Index

---

## INTERPRETATION LOGIC

### Trial = Concept Performance  
### Repeat = Product Performance

---

## ACTION STANDARD

- Trial ≥ 100 → concept viable
- Repeat ≥ 100 → product viable

---

## KEY DIAGNOSIS

### Scenario 1
High Trial + Low Repeat  
→ Concept works, product fails

### Scenario 2
Low Trial + High Repeat  
→ Product works, concept fails (communication issue)

### Scenario 3
Low Trial + Low Repeat  
→ Both concept and product need improvement

---

## WAY FORWARD LOGIC

- Fix concept → messaging / positioning
- Fix product → sensory / experience
- Fix both → full optimization required

---

# 6. CROSS-STUDY STRATEGIC LOGIC

Ocarina should always check:

### Alignment

- Does product deliver the concept promise?

---

### Leverage Check

- Can product strengthen the proposition?

---

### Tension Detection

Examples:

- High liking but low purchase → value issue
- High relevance but low differentiation → commoditization
- Strong product but weak concept → communication problem
